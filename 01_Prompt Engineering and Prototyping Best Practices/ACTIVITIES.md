🧑‍🤝‍🧑❓ Discussion Question #1:  
What are some limitations of vibe checking as an evaluation tool?

Vibe checking is a good start for evaluating an application; however, it can be very subjective. For example, a professional and formal tone might mean one thing to someone, but another to someone else, leading to inconsistency. 

It also can be very limited and there might be some edge cases that the user might not think about when testing. Furthermore, there might be logical errors that the user doesn’t realize. The response could sound correct, but might contain misleading or wrong information.

Finally, vibe checks don’t scale very well. Different evaluators might reach different conclusions from their vibe checks, depending on their mood or context. Human judgement can also change over time, making the results inconsistent and hard to replicate.
